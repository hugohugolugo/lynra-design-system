Brand hierarchy

Exemplified through a template village Polaris.

01

Lynra is the system

It represents the operating method, the standards and the calibrated structure applied across all villages.

02

Polaris is the site

It is the first named village within the system. Future locations will follow the same pattern.

03

The village name is primary in place-specific contexts

Polaris Village functions as the headline identifier on-site and in local communication.

04

Lynra is present as the underlying framework

Its role is expressed through the symbol and visual discipline, not through dominant labeling.

05

No verbal lockups

Avoid combinations such as “Polaris Village by Lynra” or “Lynra Polaris.”
The relationship is architectural, not branded.

06

Visual hierarchy

Polaris Village → primary
Lynra symbol → secondary, consistent, quiet

07

System coherence

Every village has its own name.
Lynra remains the unifying structure across all sites.

08

Part of Strawberry

Lynra is a part of Strawberry Living. When we talk to stakeholders or new prospects, this should be highlighted.
