Slide templates

Every template is the deck's 1920×1080 canvas shown at 1280×720 — open one and use the browser at full width. Brandbook pages (Slide*) come from the brandbook Figma frames; Keynote* is the spoken format, Sendout* the read-alone format. Serve the repository over HTTP (npx serve .) so fonts and assets load.

  • SlideTitle — The deck cover: a full-bleed village photo with the white wordmark on the right half — no meta strip, no other words.
  • SlideIndex — The index: the title at left and three columns of chapter names — Funnel Display SemiBold 32px heads over 18px Inter Tight items.
  • SlideSectionOpener — Section opener: the section word at 816px in Clay bleeding off a Granite canvas, and an Ash peg-board card with the title and a lede.
  • SlideContent — The deck's workhorse: a 90px title at left, a 36px statement in the right column — both starting at y = 386.
  • SlideColumns — Title at left, three text columns at right: 24px SemiBold heads over 16px body, columns 261px wide at x = 979, 1279 and 1578.
  • SlideCards — Title and a one-line subtitle at left; a grid of white cards at right — numbered, 276px wide, 30px padding, 16px gaps.
  • SlideStat — Data visualisation as the brandbook draws it: stat tiles in Umber, Red Ember and Aluminium with 84px figures, a pie and a bar chart in the palette.
  • SlideBento — The boxed layout for branded overviews: a Red Ember statement tile, a white quote card and a photo tile, an Aluminium tile bleeding off the foot.
  • SlideBigQuote — A quote on Red Ember with an Umber attribution, a portrait photo and a Granite logo tile at right, a white detail card bleeding off the foot.
  • SlideWayfinding — The wayfinding system on a white canvas: peg-board signs in Ash, Aluminium, Obsidian and Red Ember with Funnel Display place names and → arrows.
  • SlideColors — The palette page: a 90px title and a short intro at left, two rows of 262px swatches with their HEX, RGB, CMYK and Pantone values.
  • SlideTwoTiles — Two 861×618 white tiles side by side with a name and description under each — the page for lockups, applications and photographs.
  • KeynoteTitle — Keynote title: a full-bleed photograph, the Ash symbol at the margin and the 90px white title a space-6 (64px) to its right, both ending 160px above the foot.
  • KeynoteStatement — The keynote's core slide: a photograph, a 26px label (Vision:) and one 90px white line on column 4, ending 160px above the foot.
  • KeynoteQuote — The keynote's pause slide: Aluminium ground, a 26px label and a 90px line in Obsidian on column 4, vertically centred.
  • KeynoteLogo — The keynote's logo slide: the Ash horizontal lockup, 1119px wide, centred on a full-bleed photograph.
  • KeynoteDiagram — A process as cards on the dot grid: a 90px title at left, three columns of white question cards, the Red Ember result and Granite commitments at right.
  • KeynoteCompare — Two entities as white panels joined by a Red Ember arrow on the dot grid — text and icon facts inside, Granite fact cards below.
  • SendoutCover — Sendout cover: a photograph at 80% over Aluminium with the 110px title in white at the margin, ending 80px above the foot, the meta strip on top.
  • SendoutText — Sendout text page: a 110px title at y = 160, 21px running text in a five-column measure below it, one photograph on columns 7–12.
  • SendoutColumns — Sendout two-column page: two 110px titles on columns 1 and 7 at y = 160, numbered 21px text under each in a five-column measure.
