UX and design

Tech

  1. Modern interface standard. All user-facing interfaces must use current design patterns and interaction models, aligned with contemporary consumer-grade digital products.
  2. Performance. Page load time must not exceed 1.5 seconds on standard mobile connections; perceived performance is supported through skeleton states, progressive loading and optimistic updates.
  3. Design-led user experience. Navigation, hierarchy and flow follow established UX principles. Every screen shows intentional layout and purposeful use of space; no default framework components in production.
  4. Unified visual system. Web, app, notifications and embedded widgets share one design language — typography, colour, spacing, iconography and components consistent on every surface.
  1. Interoperability. Solutions that solve a variety of problems from the same interface: the guest opens doors, books laundry slots and orders food pick-up in one app; staff keep all guest communication in one system. Documented APIs whenever relevant.
  2. Scalability. Lynra is on a growth journey and chooses partners who plan for it: a system built for 50 buildings today needs a viable plan for 600.
  3. Flexibility. We learn along the way, experiment and iterate — a mindset we hope you share, including bespoke solutions outside your current routines when asked.
